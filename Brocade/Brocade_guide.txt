Above parameters explained:
• enc_out - Number of encoding error outside of frames received (Rx).
• crc err - Number of frames with CRC errors received (Rx).
• Lr_in - Link reset in (primitive sequence), does not apply to FL_Port.
• Lr_out - Link reset out (primitive sequence), does not apply to FL_Port.
• Ols_in - Offline reset in (primitive sequence), does not apply to FL_Port.
• Ols_out - Offline reset out (primitive sequence), does not apply to FL_Port.

Zone Creation Procedure
============================================================================

alicreate "Servername_HBA00","server wwpn"
zonecreate "zone_name","server_alias;storage_alias"
cfgadd "SE9990_A","Zone_name"
cfgsave
cfgenable SE9990_A




Deleting existing Zone
=============================================================================
1.Move zone to defined configuration and remove zone members from the zone.

 
cfgremove "cfgname","Zone_name"
zoneremove "zone_name","host_alias;storage_alias"
cfgsave
cfgenable cfgname

 


2. delete zone.

zonedelete "Zone_name"
cfgsave
cfgenable cfgname

 

3.delete host alias
alidelete “host_alias”





HBA replacements/Same procedure for alias renaming also
=================================================================================

1.first remove zone from effective conf
cfgremove "testfcsw1_cfg","Zone_name"

2.Removing old alias from zone
aliRemove "Zone_name","old_alias/WWPN"

3.Deleting old alias which we removed
alidelete "old_server_alias"

4.creating new alias with new wwpn
alicreate "server_name","new_WWPN"

5.adding new alias to zone
aliadd "zone_name","server_name"


cfgadd "SE9990_A","Zone_name"
cfgsave
cfgenable SE9990_A
